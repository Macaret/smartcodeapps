Learn to Read — Support

Welcome to the support page for Learn to Read, the app that helps preschoolers learn to read in Romanian.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What age is the app designed for?
    Learn to Read is designed for preschoolers who are just beginning their reading journey.
  • Is the app free?
    The first letters are completely free. Full access to all letters and games is unlocked through a one-time purchase, with no subscription required.
  • Does the app work without internet?
    Most content works offline after downloading. However, the voice recognition feature requires an active internet connection.
  • What devices is the app available on?
    Learn to Read is available on iPhone and iPad running iOS 16.6 or later, as well as on Mac with macOS.
  • I purchased the app on one device and want to access it on another. What should I do?
    Open the app on your new device, go to the payment screen and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
