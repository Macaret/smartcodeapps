Progressive learning path

1

Letter Recognition

Children start by learning individual letters with phonetic instructions and visual cues. Voice recognition helps them practice pronunciation in a judgment-free environment.

  • Letter identification
  • Sound association
  • Voice feedback
2

Syllable Formation

Once letters are familiar, children learn how sounds combine into syllables. This intermediate step builds confidence before tackling full words.

  • Vowel combinations
  • Syllable patterns
  • Sound blending
3

Word Reading

Children apply their foundational knowledge to read complete words. Success reinforces learning and builds reading confidence and interest in literature.

  • Common words
  • Sentence formation
  • Reading exercises
4

Reading Sentences & Stories

The final stage brings reading to life through engaging short stories and sentence construction. Children practice reading fluency and comprehension with real narratives.

  • Short stories
  • Sentence comprehension
  • Reading fluency
